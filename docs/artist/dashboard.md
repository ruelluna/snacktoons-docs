# Dashboard & Analytics

## Overview

The Artist Dashboard is your command center for monitoring performance, tracking earnings, and understanding your audience. This comprehensive overview provides real-time insights into your comic's success.

## Dashboard Components

### Key Statistics

The dashboard displays six primary metrics at the top:

#### 1. Total Comics
- **What it shows**: Number of comic series you've created
- **Icon**: Book open icon
- **Color**: Primary (purple)
- **Description**: "Comics created by you"

#### 2. Total Chapters
- **What it shows**: Combined number of chapters across all your comics
- **Icon**: Document text icon
- **Color**: Info (blue)
- **Description**: "Chapters published by you"

#### 3. Total Reads
- **What it shows**: Cumulative read count for all your content
- **Icon**: Eye icon
- **Color**: Primary (purple)
- **Description**: "Total reads across all your comics"

#### 4. Unique Readers
- **What it shows**: Number of individual users who have read your work
- **Icon**: User group icon
- **Color**: Success (green)
- **Description**: "Unique users who have read your comics"

#### 5. Coins Earned
- **What it shows**: Revenue generated from paid chapter unlocks
- **Icon**: Currency dollar icon
- **Color**: Warning (yellow)
- **Description**: "Coins earned from paid chapter unlocks"

#### 6. Average Completion Rate
- **What it shows**: Percentage of readers who finish your comics
- **Icon**: Check circle icon
- **Color**: Success (green)
- **Description**: "Average percent of users who finish your comics"

## Understanding Your Metrics

### Readership Analytics

#### Total Reads vs Unique Readers
- **Total Reads**: Includes multiple reads from the same user
- **Unique Readers**: Counts each user only once, regardless of how many times they read
- **Insight**: High total reads with low unique readers indicates loyal, returning fans

#### Completion Rate
- **Calculation**: (Users who finished / Total users who started) × 100
- **Target**: Aim for 60%+ completion rate for strong engagement
- **Improvement**: Analyze where readers drop off to optimize content

### Revenue Tracking

#### Coin Earnings
- **Source**: Paid chapter unlocks by readers
- **Calculation**: Sum of all coins spent on your chapters
- **Conversion**: Coins are converted to USD for payouts
- **Tracking**: Monitor daily/weekly trends to understand peak reading times

## Performance Insights

### What the Numbers Tell You

#### High Total Reads, Low Unique Readers
- **Meaning**: Loyal audience, strong series
- **Action**: Focus on retention and completion rates

#### High Unique Readers, Low Total Reads
- **Meaning**: Good discovery, but low retention
- **Action**: Improve chapter quality and pacing

#### Low Completion Rate
- **Meaning**: Readers lose interest before finishing
- **Action**: Review chapter structure and content quality

#### High Coin Earnings
- **Meaning**: Strong monetization strategy
- **Action**: Consider increasing chapter frequency

## Troubleshooting Dashboard Issues

### Common Problems

#### Statistics Not Updating
- **Cause**: Browser cache or network issues
- **Solution**: Refresh the page or clear browser cache

#### Missing Data
- **Cause**: Recent content not yet processed
- **Solution**: Wait 24-48 hours for data to populate

#### Inconsistent Numbers
- **Cause**: Different calculation methods or time zones
- **Solution**: Check the time period and calculation basis
