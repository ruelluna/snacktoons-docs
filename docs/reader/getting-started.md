# Getting Started

## Welcome to the Comic Platform

Welcome to our comic reading platform! This guide will help you get started with discovering, reading, and enjoying comics. Whether you're new to digital comics or a seasoned reader, we'll walk you through everything you need to know.

## Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Registration

1. **Visit the Platform**: Navigate to our website
2. **Click Register**: Find the "Register" or "Sign Up" button
3. **Fill in Your Information**:
   - **Full Name**: Your display name on the platform
   - **Email Address**: Your primary contact email
   - **Password**: Create a strong, secure password
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password
4. **Accept Terms**: Read and accept the Terms of Service
5. **Complete Registration**: Click "Register" to create your account

### Step 2: Email Verification

After registration, you'll receive a verification email:

1. **Check Your Email**: Look for the verification email in your inbox (and spam folder)
2. **Click Verification Link**: Click the link in the email to verify your account
3. **Account Activated**: Your account is now ready to use

### Step 3: Complete Your Profile

Once verified, complete your profile:

1. **Profile Photo**: Upload a profile picture (optional)
2. **Preferences**: Set your reading preferences
3. **Language**: Choose your preferred language
4. **Content Filter**: Set adult content preferences

## Reading Your First Comic

### Finding a Comic

1. **Browse Categories**: Click on a genre that interests you
2. **Use Search**: Search for specific titles or authors
3. **Check Recommendations**: Look at personalized suggestions
4. **Explore Trending**: See what's popular with other readers

### Starting to Read

1. **Select a Comic**: Click on a comic that interests you
2. **Read the Description**: Learn about the story and characters
3. **Check Chapter List**: See available chapters
4. **Start Reading**: Click on the first chapter to begin

### Understanding Chapter Access

#### Free Chapters
- **Available to All**: No account required
- **Limited Selection**: Some chapters are free to attract readers
- **Sample Content**: Get a taste of the story before purchasing

#### Paid Chapters
- **Coin Purchase**: Use coins to unlock individual chapters
- **Subscription Access**: Subscribers can read all chapters
- **Premium Content**: Exclusive or special chapters

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Registration Problems
- **Email Not Received**: Check spam folder, request resend
- **Verification Issues**: Click link again, contact support
- **Password Problems**: Use password reset feature

#### Reading Issues
- **Images Not Loading**: Check internet connection, refresh page
- **Progress Not Saving**: Ensure you're logged in
- **Access Denied**: Check if content requires purchase

#### Payment Issues
- **Coin Purchase**: Verify payment method, check transaction
- **Subscription**: Confirm billing information
- **Access Problems**: Contact support for billing issues

### Getting Help
- **Support Tickets**: Create detailed tickets with screenshots
- **Community Help**: Ask other users in forums
- **Documentation**: Check our help guides
