# Managing Artists

This guide explains how to manage artists in the admin panel. As an administrator, you can create, view, edit, and manage artist accounts and their payout settings.

## Accessing the Artists Section

1. Log in to the admin panel with your administrator credentials
2. Navigate to the main sidebar navigation
3. Click on **Artists** to access the artist management interface

## Viewing Artists

The Artists page displays a table with all artist accounts in the system. The table includes information such as:

- Artist name
- Email address
- Other relevant details

You can:
- **Search** for artists by name or email using the search bar
- **Sort** the table by clicking on column headers
- **Filter** artists based on various criteria

## Creating a New Artist

To create a new artist account:

1. Click the **New Artist** button at the top of the Artists page
2. Fill in the required information:
   - **Name**: Enter the full name of the artist
   - **Email**: Enter a valid email address
   - **Profile Photo**: Upload a profile photo (optional)
3. Configure the artist's payout settings:
   - **Subscription Payout Percentage**: Set the percentage of subscription revenue the artist receives
   - **Coin Payout Percentage**: Set the percentage of coin revenue the artist receives
4. Enter the artist's payment information (optional):
   - Bank account details
   - PayPal email
   - Tax ID
5. Click **Create** to add the new artist

## Viewing Artist Details

To view detailed information about an artist:

1. Find the artist in the table
2. Click on the artist's name or the **View** button

The artist detail page shows:
- Profile information (name, email, profile photo)
- Payout settings
- Payment information
- Associated comics

## Editing an Artist

To edit an existing artist:

1. Find the artist in the table
2. Click the **Edit** button in the actions column
3. Update the artist information as needed:
   - Profile details
   - Payout percentages
   - Payment information
4. Click **Save** to apply your changes

## Managing Artist Payout Settings

Artist payout settings determine how much revenue the artist receives from subscriptions and coin purchases:

1. Navigate to the artist's edit page
2. Locate the **Artist Payout Settings** section
3. Configure the following settings:
   - **Subscription Payout Percentage**: The percentage of subscription revenue allocated to the artist
   - **Coin Payout Percentage**: The percentage of coin purchase revenue allocated to the artist
4. Click **Save** to apply the changes

Default payout percentages are applied if no custom values are set.

## Managing Artist Payment Information

To manage an artist's payment information:

1. Navigate to the artist's edit page
2. Locate the payment information fields:
   - **Bank Account Name**
   - **Bank Account Number**
   - **Bank Name**
   - **Bank Swift Code**
   - **PayPal Email**
   - **Tax ID**
3. Update the information as needed
4. Click **Save** to apply the changes

This information is used for processing payouts to the artist.

## Viewing Artist Comics

To view comics associated with an artist:

1. Navigate to the artist's detail page
2. Scroll down to the **Comics** section
3. Browse the list of comics created by the artist

You can click on any comic to view its details or manage it.

## Best Practices

- Verify artist information before creating accounts
- Set fair and consistent payout percentages across artists
- Ensure payment information is complete and accurate before processing payouts
- Regularly review artist accounts and their associated content
- Communicate any changes to payout percentages to artists in advance
