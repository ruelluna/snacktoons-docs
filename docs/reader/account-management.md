# Account Management

## Overview

Your account is your gateway to the comic platform. This guide covers everything you need to know about managing your profile, security settings, and account preferences.

## Accessing Your Account

### Profile Section

1. **Navigate to Profile**: Click on your profile picture or name in the top navigation
2. **Account Settings**: Select "Profile" or "Account Settings"
3. **Manage Information**: Update your personal details and preferences

### Account Dashboard

Your account dashboard provides quick access to:
- **Profile Information**: Personal details and settings
- **Reading History**: Comics and chapters you've read
- **Favorites**: Comics you've saved
- **Purchases**: Content you've bought
- **Settings**: Account and privacy preferences

## Profile Management

### Basic Information

#### Personal Details
- **Name**: Your display name on the platform
- **Email**: Primary contact email (used for login)
- **Profile Photo**: Optional profile picture
- **Bio**: Brief description about yourself (optional)

#### Updating Information

1. Open **Profile** from the top navigation (profile picture or name menu)
2. In **Profile Information**, update **Name** and/or **Email**
3. To add or change your photo, click **Select A New Photo**, choose an image (JPG or PNG, max 1 MB), then click **Save**
4. To remove a photo, click **Remove Photo**, then **Save**
5. Some email changes may require verification before the new address is active

### Privacy Settings

#### Profile Visibility
- **Public Profile**: Other users can see your profile
- **Private Profile**: Only you can see your profile
- **Reading History**: Control who can see what you've read
- **Favorites**: Manage visibility of your favorite comics

#### Data Sharing
- **Analytics**: Help improve the platform (anonymous)
- **Recommendations**: Allow personalized content suggestions
- **Social Features**: Enable comments and interactions
- **Marketing**: Receive updates and promotions (optional)

## Security Settings

### Password Management

#### Changing Your Password
1. **Security Section**: Go to "Security" or "Password"
2. **Current Password**: Enter your current password
3. **New Password**: Create a strong new password
4. **Confirm Password**: Re-enter the new password
5. **Save Changes**: Update your password

#### Password Requirements
- **Minimum Length**: At least 8 characters
- **Complexity**: Include letters, numbers, and symbols
- **Uniqueness**: Don't reuse passwords from other sites
- **Regular Updates**: Change your password periodically

### Two-Factor Authentication

#### Setting Up 2FA
1. **Enable 2FA**: Go to security settings
2. **Choose Method**: Select authenticator app or SMS
3. **Scan QR Code**: Use your authenticator app
4. **Verify Setup**: Enter the generated code
5. **Backup Codes**: Save recovery codes securely

#### Managing 2FA
- **Disable 2FA**: Turn off if needed (not recommended)
- **Change Method**: Switch between app and SMS
- **Recovery Codes**: Generate new backup codes
- **Device Management**: Remove trusted devices

### Login Security

#### Session Management
- **Active Sessions**: View all logged-in devices
- **Logout Remotely**: Sign out from other devices
- **Trusted Devices**: Remember devices for easier login
- **Login History**: Review recent login activity

#### Security Alerts
- **New Device Login**: Get notified of unrecognized logins
- **Password Changes**: Receive alerts for password updates
- **Suspicious Activity**: Notifications for unusual behavior
- **Account Lockouts**: Alerts for failed login attempts

## Account Recovery

### Lost Password

#### Password Reset Process
1. **Forgot Password**: Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. **Enter Email**: Provide your registered email address
3. **Check Email**: Look for reset link in your inbox
4. **Create New Password**: Set a new secure password
5. **Login**: Use your new password to access account

#### Alternative Recovery
- **Security Questions**: Answer pre-set questions
- **Phone Verification**: Use SMS for account recovery
- **Support Contact**: Contact support for manual recovery
- **Account Verification**: Provide proof of identity

### Account Lockout

#### Common Causes
- **Too Many Attempts**: Multiple failed login attempts
- **Suspicious Activity**: Unusual login patterns
- **Security Breach**: Potential account compromise
- **Policy Violation**: Terms of service violations

#### Recovery Steps
1. **Wait Period**: Allow lockout period to expire
2. **Contact Support**: Reach out for immediate assistance
3. **Verify Identity**: Provide required documentation
4. **Account Review**: Support team reviews your account
5. **Access Restored**: Account unlocked after verification

## Account Deletion

### Before Deleting

#### Data Backup
- **Favorites**: Save list of favorite comics
- **Purchases**: Download purchased content

#### Considerations
- **Permanent Action**: Account deletion cannot be undone
- **Content Loss**: Lose access to purchased content
- **Data Removal**: All personal data will be deleted
- **No Refunds**: Unused coins or subscriptions may be lost

### Deletion Process

#### Request Deletion
1. **Account Settings**: Go to account management
2. **Delete Account**: Find account deletion option
3. **Confirm Intent**: Acknowledge permanent deletion
4. **Verify Identity**: Provide password or verification
5. **Submit Request**: Complete deletion process

#### Processing Time
- **Immediate Deactivation**: Account access disabled
- **Data Processing**: Personal data removal (30 days)
- **Final Deletion**: Complete account removal
- **Confirmation**: Email confirmation of deletion

## Best Practices

### Account Security
- **Strong Passwords**: Use unique, complex passwords
- **Regular Updates**: Change password periodically
- **Two-Factor Auth**: Enable for extra security
- **Monitor Activity**: Check login history regularly

### Privacy Protection
- **Limit Sharing**: Be careful with personal information
- **Review Settings**: Regularly check privacy preferences
- **Secure Devices**: Keep devices and browsers updated
- **Logout Properly**: Sign out from shared devices

### Data Management

- **Clean History**: Remove unwanted reading history
- **Update Information**: Keep profile details current
- **Review Permissions**: Check app and service permissions

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Problems
- **Forgot Password**: Use password reset feature
- **Account Locked**: Contact support for assistance
- **Email Issues**: Check spam folder, verify email
- **Browser Problems**: Clear cache, try different browser

#### Profile Issues
- **Photo Upload**: Check file size and format
- **Name Changes**: Some restrictions may apply
- **Email Updates**: Requires verification
- **Settings Not Saving**: Check internet connection

#### Security Concerns
- **Suspicious Activity**: Report immediately to support
- **Unauthorized Access**: Change password, enable 2FA
- **Phishing Attempts**: Never share passwords via email
- **Account Compromise**: Contact support immediately

### Getting Help
- **Support Tickets**: Create detailed support requests
- **Help Documentation**: Check our comprehensive guides
- **Community Forums**: Ask other users for advice
- **Direct Contact**: Reach out for urgent security issues
