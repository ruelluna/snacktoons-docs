# Getting Started

## Welcome to the Artist Panel

The Artist Panel is your central hub for creating, managing, and monetizing your comics on our platform. This guide will walk you through the essential steps to get started.

## Account Registration

### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Navigate to the Artist Panel registration page
2. Fill in the required information:
   - **Full Name**: Your display name on the platform
   - **Email Address**: Your primary contact email
   - **Password**: Create a strong, secure password
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password
3. Read and accept the Terms of Service
4. Click "Register" to create your account

### Step 2: Email Verification

After registration, you'll receive a verification email:

1. Check your email inbox (and spam folder)
2. Click the verification link in the email
3. Your account will be automatically verified
4. You can now log in to the Artist Panel

### Step 3: Complete Your Profile

Once logged in, complete your artist profile:

1. **Profile Photo**: Upload a professional profile picture
2. **Bio**: Add a brief description about yourself and your work
3. **Social Links**: Include links to your social media profiles
4. **Payment Information**: Set up your payout details (see Payout Settings)

## First Steps

### Understanding the Dashboard

Your dashboard provides an overview of your activity:

- **Total Comics**: Number of comics you've created
- **Total Chapters**: Total chapters across all your comics
- **Total Reads**: Combined read count for all your content
- **Unique Readers**: Number of individual users who have read your work
- **Coins Earned**: Revenue from paid chapter unlocks
- **Completion Rate**: Average percentage of users who finish your comics

### Navigation Overview

The Artist Panel is organized into several main sections:

- **Dashboard**: Overview and statistics
- **Comics**: Manage your comic series
- **Chapters**: Individual chapter management
- **Payouts**: Revenue and payment tracking
- **Support**: Help and ticket system

## Best Practices

- **Complete Your Profile**: A complete profile builds trust with readers
- **Use High-Quality Images**: Ensure your cover and chapter images are clear and professional
- **Regular Updates**: Maintain a consistent publishing schedule
- **Engage with Readers**: Respond to comments and feedback
- **Monitor Analytics**: Use dashboard data to understand your audience

If you need assistance:

## Content Guidelines

Before publishing your first comic, familiarize yourself with:

- **Content Standards**: Platform policies and community guidelines
- **Image Requirements**: Technical specifications for uploads
- **Copyright**: Ensure you have rights to all content
