# Social Features

## Overview

The platform's social features allow you to connect with other readers, share your thoughts on comics, and participate in the community. This guide covers commenting, rating, sharing, and engaging with the comic community.

## Comments and Discussion

### Chapter Comments

#### Adding Comments
1. **While Reading**: Navigate to any chapter page
2. **Find Comment Section**: Scroll to the comments area
3. **Write Comment**: Share your thoughts and reactions
4. **Submit Comment**: Post your comment for others to see

#### Comment Features
- **Real-time Updates**: See new comments as they appear
- **Reply System**: Respond to other users' comments
- **Like Comments**: Show appreciation for good comments
- **Comment Threading**: Follow conversation threads

### Comment Management

#### Your Comments
- **View History**: See all comments you've made
- **Edit Comments**: Modify your comments within time limit
- **Delete Comments**: Remove comments you no longer want
- **Comment Privacy**: Control who can see your comments

#### Comment Moderation
- **Report Comments**: Flag inappropriate content
- **Community Guidelines**: Follow platform rules
- **Moderation Actions**: Understand comment removal
- **Appeal Process**: Contest moderation decisions

### Discussion Etiquette

#### Best Practices
- **Be Respectful**: Treat others with kindness and respect
- **Stay On Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the comic
- **Avoid Spoilers**: Use spoiler tags for plot reveals
- **Constructive Feedback**: Provide helpful, thoughtful comments

#### What to Avoid
- **Hate Speech**: No discriminatory or offensive language
- **Spam**: Don't post repetitive or irrelevant content
- **Personal Attacks**: Don't insult or harass other users
- **Copyright Violations**: Don't share copyrighted material

## Community Engagement

### Community Engagement

### User Profiles

#### Profile Information
- **Public Profile**: Share information about yourself
- **Reading Preferences**: Show your favorite genres
- **Reading Stats**: Display your reading activity
- **Social Links**: Connect other social media accounts

#### Profile Customization
- **Profile Picture**: Upload a personal avatar
- **Bio Section**: Write about your reading interests
- **Favorite Comics**: Showcase your top picks

## Sharing and Recommendations

### Sharing Comics

#### Social Sharing
- **Direct Links**: Share comic links with friends
- **Social Media**: Post to Facebook, Twitter, Instagram
- **Messaging Apps**: Share via WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.
- **Email Sharing**: Send recommendations via email

#### Sharing Features
- **Custom Messages**: Add personal notes to shares
- **Share Statistics**: Track how often comics are shared
- **Viral Tracking**: See which comics are trending
- **Share Analytics**: Monitor your sharing activity

### Recommendations

#### Giving Recommendations
- **Personal Picks**: Recommend your favorite comics
- **Genre Suggestions**: Suggest comics by category
- **Mood-based**: Recommend based on reading mood
- **Similar Comics**: Suggest comics like others

#### Receiving Recommendations
- **Algorithm Suggestions**: Get AI-powered recommendations
- **Friend Recommendations**: See what friends suggest
- **Community Picks**: Discover popular community choices
- **Expert Recommendations**: Get suggestions from critics

## Privacy and Safety

### Privacy Settings

#### Comment Privacy
- **Public Comments**: Visible to all users
- **Friends Only**: Visible only to your connections
- **Private Comments**: Visible only to you
- **Comment Moderation**: Control who can comment on your posts

#### Profile Privacy
- **Public Profile**: Visible to all users
- **Friends Only**: Visible only to your connections
- **Private Profile**: Visible only to you
- **Custom Privacy**: Set specific privacy rules

### Safety Features

#### Content Moderation
- **Report System**: Report inappropriate content
- **Block Users**: Prevent specific users from interacting with you
- **Mute Users**: Hide content from specific users
- **Content Filtering**: Filter out unwanted content

#### Safety Guidelines
- **Personal Information**: Don't share sensitive personal details
- **Online Safety**: Be cautious with online interactions
- **Reporting Abuse**: Report harassment or inappropriate behavior
- **Community Support**: Seek help from moderators when needed

## Best Practices

### Community Engagement
- **Be Active**: Regularly participate in community discussions
- **Be Helpful**: Provide useful recommendations and feedback
- **Be Respectful**: Treat all community members with respect
- **Be Authentic**: Share genuine thoughts and experiences

### Content Creation
- **Quality Comments**: Write thoughtful, engaging comments
- **Helpful Reviews**: Provide detailed, constructive reviews
- **Creative Sharing**: Share interesting content and perspectives
- **Consistent Participation**: Maintain regular community presence

### Social Networking
- **Build Connections**: Connect with like-minded readers
- **Share Discoveries**: Help others find great comics
- **Participate in Events**: Join community activities
- **Support Others**: Encourage and support fellow readers
