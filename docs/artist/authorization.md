# Artist Panel Authorization

## Access Requirements

To access the Artist Panel, you must have an artist account with the following requirements:

- **Valid Email Address**: Must be verified through email verification
- **Artist Role**: Account must be assigned the 'artist' role
- **Terms Agreement**: Must agree to the platform's terms of service

## Login Process

1. Navigate to the Artist Panel login page
2. Enter your registered email address and password
3. Complete any required verification steps
4. Accept the terms of service if prompted

## Email Verification

After registration, you must verify your email address:

1. Check your email for the verification link
2. Click the verification link to confirm your account
3. Return to the Artist Panel to complete setup

## Account Security

- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication if available
- Keep your login credentials secure
- Log out when using shared devices

## Support Access

If you encounter login issues:

1. Use the "Forgot Password" feature
2. Contact support through the ticket system
3. Ensure your email is verified and active
