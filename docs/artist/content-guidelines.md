# Content Guidelines

## Overview

Content guidelines ensure quality, appropriate content on the platform while protecting artists and readers. This guide covers platform policies, content standards, and best practices for creating successful comics.

## Platform Policies

### Content Standards

#### Quality Requirements
- **Art Quality**: High-resolution, clear, professional artwork
- **Story Coherence**: Logical narrative progression and character development
- **Technical Standards**: Proper formatting, file quality, and presentation
- **Original Content**: Original work or properly licensed material

#### Age Appropriateness
- **All Ages**: Content suitable for readers of all ages
- **Teen**: Content appropriate for teenagers (13+)
- **Mature**: Content for adults (18+) with appropriate warnings
- **Adult**: Explicit content requiring age verification

### Prohibited Content

#### Illegal Content
- **Copyright Violations**: Unauthorized use of copyrighted material
- **Trademark Infringement**: Unauthorized use of trademarks
- **Defamation**: False statements harming reputation
- **Privacy Violations**: Unauthorized use of personal information

#### Harmful Content
- **Violence**: Excessive or gratuitous violence
- **Hate Speech**: Content promoting discrimination or hatred
- **Harassment**: Content targeting or harassing individuals
- **Self-Harm**: Content promoting self-harm or suicide

#### Inappropriate Content
- **Explicit Sexual Content**: Pornographic or sexually explicit material
- **Gore**: Excessive blood, gore, or graphic violence
- **Drug Use**: Promotion of illegal drug use
- **Criminal Activity**: Glorification of criminal behavior

### Community Guidelines

#### Respectful Behavior
- **Reader Respect**: Treat readers with respect and dignity
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Be mindful of cultural differences
- **Inclusive Content**: Create content that welcomes diverse audiences
- **Professional Conduct**: Maintain professional behavior in interactions

#### Content Responsibility
- **Accurate Information**: Provide accurate and truthful content
- **Appropriate Warnings**: Warn readers about sensitive content
- **Content Ratings**: Accurately rate content for appropriate audiences
- **Quality Commitment**: Maintain high standards in all content

## Content Creation Best Practices

### Story Development

#### Narrative Structure
- **Clear Plot**: Develop coherent storylines with clear progression
- **Character Development**: Create engaging, well-developed characters
- **Conflict Resolution**: Include meaningful conflicts and resolutions
- **Pacing**: Balance action, dialogue, and character moments

#### Genre Considerations
- **Genre Conventions**: Understand and follow genre expectations
- **Audience Expectations**: Meet reader expectations for your genre
- **Innovation**: Add unique elements while respecting genre traditions
- **Cross-Genre**: Blend genres thoughtfully and effectively

### Visual Quality

#### Art Standards
- **Consistent Style**: Maintain consistent visual style throughout
- **High Resolution**: Use high-quality images (minimum 800px width)
- **Clear Composition**: Ensure clear, readable panel layouts
- **Professional Quality**: Strive for professional-level artwork

#### Technical Requirements
- **File Formats**: Use JPG, PNG, or WebP formats
- **File Sizes**: Optimize for web viewing (under 2MB per image)
- **Aspect Ratios**: Maintain consistent aspect ratios
- **Color Quality**: Ensure good color reproduction and contrast

### Character Development

#### Character Creation
- **Distinct Personalities**: Create unique, memorable characters
- **Character Arcs**: Develop characters over time
- **Relatable Traits**: Include relatable human qualities
- **Diverse Representation**: Include diverse characters and perspectives

#### Character Consistency
- **Visual Consistency**: Maintain consistent character appearance
- **Personality Consistency**: Keep character behavior consistent
- **Voice Consistency**: Maintain consistent character dialogue
- **Growth and Change**: Allow characters to develop naturally

## Content Categories and Genres

### Popular Genres

#### Action and Adventure
- **Requirements**: Dynamic action sequences, engaging plots
- **Best Practices**: Clear action flow, compelling stakes
- **Audience**: Broad appeal, various age groups
- **Examples**: Superhero, martial arts, fantasy adventure

#### Romance
- **Requirements**: Emotional depth, character relationships
- **Best Practices**: Authentic emotions, gradual development
- **Audience**: Primarily teen and adult readers
- **Examples**: Contemporary romance, historical romance

#### Fantasy and Sci-Fi
- **Requirements**: Rich world-building, consistent rules
- **Best Practices**: Clear magic/technology systems
- **Audience**: Broad appeal, imaginative readers
- **Examples**: High fantasy, space opera, urban fantasy

#### Comedy
- **Requirements**: Humor, timing, relatable situations
- **Best Practices**: Varied humor types, character-driven comedy
- **Audience**: All ages, depending on content
- **Examples**: Slapstick, situational comedy, parody

### Content Rating Guidelines

#### All Ages
- **Content**: Suitable for children and families
- **Language**: Clean, appropriate language
- **Violence**: Minimal or cartoon violence
- **Themes**: Positive, educational, or entertaining themes

#### Teen (13+)
- **Content**: Appropriate for teenagers
- **Language**: Mild language, no profanity
- **Violence**: Moderate action, no graphic violence
- **Themes**: Coming-of-age, friendship, adventure

#### Mature (18+)
- **Content**: Adult themes and situations
- **Language**: Strong language may be included
- **Violence**: More intense action and violence
- **Themes**: Complex adult relationships and situations

#### Adult (18+ with verification)
- **Content**: Explicit adult content
- **Requirements**: Age verification required
- **Warnings**: Clear content warnings
- **Restrictions**: Limited discoverability

## Technical Guidelines

### Image Requirements

#### Quality Standards
- **Resolution**: Minimum 800px width, recommended 1200px+
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP formats
- **Compression**: Optimize for web without quality loss
- **Color Space**: Use sRGB color space for web compatibility

#### Upload Guidelines
- **File Size**: Keep individual images under 2MB
- **Batch Upload**: Upload multiple images efficiently
- **Organization**: Maintain logical reading order
- **Backup**: Keep original high-resolution files

### Content Organization

#### Chapter Structure
- **Logical Flow**: Ensure smooth reading progression
- **Page Breaks**: Use natural page breaks and cliffhangers
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistent chapter length
- **Quality**: Ensure each chapter meets quality standards

#### Series Management
- **Story Arcs**: Plan and execute coherent story arcs
- **Character Development**: Develop characters across series
- **World Building**: Create consistent fictional worlds
- **Reader Engagement**: Keep readers engaged throughout series
