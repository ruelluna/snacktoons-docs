# Creating Comics

## Overview

Creating a comic series is the foundation of your content strategy. This guide will walk you through the process of setting up a new comic, from initial concept to ready-for-review status.

## Before You Start

### Planning Your Comic

Before creating your comic, consider:

- **Genre and Target Audience**: Who will read your comic?
- **Story Structure**: How many chapters do you plan?
- **Update Schedule**: How often will you publish new chapters?
- **Monetization Strategy**: Which chapters will be free vs. paid?
- **Content Rating**: Is your content appropriate for all ages?

### Required Materials

Prepare the following before creating your comic:

- **Cover Image**: High-quality cover art (recommended: 800x1200px)
- **Main Image**: Promotional image for listings (recommended: 1200x800px)
- **Thumbnail Image**: Small preview image (recommended: 400x600px)
- **Gallery Images**: Additional promotional images
- **Story Synopsis**: Brief description of your comic
- **Categories**: Relevant genre tags for discovery

## Creating a New Comic

### Step 1: Access the Comics Section

1. Log in to your Artist Panel
2. Navigate to **Comics** in the main menu
3. Click the **Create Comic** button

### Step 2: Basic Information

Fill in the essential comic details:

#### Title
- **Required**: Yes
- **Character Limit**: 255 characters
- **Best Practice**: Choose a memorable, searchable title
- **Example**: "The Dragon's Heart" or "Urban Legends: Volume 1"

#### Description
- **Required**: Yes
- **Character Limit**: 2000 characters
- **Best Practice**:
  - Hook readers in the first sentence
  - Include key characters and setting
  - Mention unique selling points
  - End with a compelling question or cliffhanger

#### Categories
- **Required**: Yes (minimum 1, maximum 5)
- **Purpose**: Helps readers discover your comic
- **Strategy**: Choose broad categories for discovery, specific ones for targeted audience

### Step 3: Visual Assets

#### Cover Image
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, WebP
- **Size**: Minimum 400x600px, recommended 800x1200px
- **Requirements**:
  - High quality, clear text
  - Represents your comic accurately
  - Appropriate for your target audience
  - No watermarks or logos

#### Main Image
- **Purpose**: Used in promotional materials and listings
- **Size**: Recommended 1200x800px (landscape)
- **Content**: Can be a scene from your comic or promotional art

#### Thumbnail Image
- **Purpose**: Small preview in lists and search results
- **Size**: Recommended 400x600px
- **Content**: Should be recognizable even at small size

#### Gallery Images
- **Optional**: Additional promotional images
- **Purpose**: Showcase your art style and story
- **Quantity**: Up to 10 images
- **Content**: Character designs, key scenes, or promotional art

### Step 4: Content Settings

#### Adult Content
- **Toggle**: Enable if your content is for mature audiences
- **Requirements**:
  - Must be 18+ content
  - Will be filtered from general search results
  - Requires age verification for readers

#### Language
- **Default**: English
- **Options**: Select the primary language of your comic
- **Impact**: Affects search and discovery for international readers

#### Status
- **Default**: Draft
- **Options**: Draft, Requested Review, In Review, Published, Unpublished
- **Workflow**: Start with Draft, request review when ready

## Advanced Settings

### SEO and Discovery

#### Meta Description
- **Purpose**: Appears in search results and social media
- **Character Limit**: 160 characters
- **Best Practice**: Include key terms and compelling description

#### Keywords
- **Purpose**: Improve search visibility
- **Format**: Comma-separated terms
- **Strategy**: Include genre, character names, themes

### Publishing Options

#### Release Schedule
- **Options**:
  - Regular schedule (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
  - Irregular updates
  - Completed series
- **Impact**: Helps readers know when to expect new chapters

#### Chapter Pricing Strategy
- **Free Chapters**: Number of free chapters to attract readers
- **Paid Chapters**: Pricing for premium content
- **Strategy**: Balance between accessibility and monetization

## Best Practices

### Title and Description

#### Compelling Titles
- **Be Specific**: "The Last Dragon Rider" vs "Dragon Story"
- **Include Genre Hints**: "Romance: Coffee Shop Love"
- **Use Keywords**: Include searchable terms naturally
- **Keep It Short**: Under 50 characters for better display

#### Effective Descriptions
- **Opening Hook**: Start with the most compelling element
- **Character Introduction**: Mention main characters briefly
- **Conflict Setup**: What drives the story forward?
- **Genre Indicators**: Make it clear what type of story this is
- **Call to Action**: End with something that makes readers want to start reading

### Visual Strategy

#### Cover Design Tips
- **Readable Text**: Ensure title is legible at thumbnail size
- **Genre Appropriate**: Visual style should match content
- **Professional Quality**: High resolution, good composition
- **Brand Consistency**: Similar style across your comics

#### Gallery Strategy
- **Showcase Variety**: Different scenes and characters
- **Highlight Quality**: Best artwork and key moments
- **Tell a Story**: Images should hint at the narrative
- **Professional Presentation**: Clean, well-composed images

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Issues
- **Incomplete Information**: Fill out all required fields
- **Poor Quality Images**: Use high-resolution, professional images
- **Misleading Descriptions**: Ensure accuracy in your synopsis
- **Inappropriate Categories**: Choose relevant, accurate tags

### Technical Problems
- **Large File Sizes**: Optimize images for web
- **Wrong Formats**: Use supported image formats
- **Missing Required Fields**: Complete all mandatory sections
- **Poor SEO**: Include relevant keywords and descriptions

## Next Steps

After creating your comic:

1. **Add Chapters**: Upload your first chapters
2. **Review Content**: Ensure quality and completeness
3. **Request Review**: Submit for platform approval
4. **Monitor Performance**: Track reads and engagement
5. **Update Regularly**: Maintain consistent publishing schedule

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Image Upload Problems
- **File Too Large**: Compress images before uploading
- **Wrong Format**: Convert to JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Upload Fails**: Check internet connection and try again

#### Save Issues
- **Form Validation**: Ensure all required fields are completed
- **Browser Problems**: Try refreshing or using a different browser
- **Session Timeout**: Log in again if session expires
