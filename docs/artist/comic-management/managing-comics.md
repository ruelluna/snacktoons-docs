# Managing Comics

## Overview

Once you've created your comic, you'll need to manage it effectively to maximize its success. This guide covers editing, status management, analytics, and optimization strategies.

## Accessing Your Comics

### Comics List View

1. Navigate to **Comics** in the main menu
2. View all your comics in a table format
3. Each comic shows:
   - **Title**: Comic name
   - **Status**: Current publication status
   - **Chapters**: Number of chapters
   - **Reads**: Total read count
   - **Actions**: Edit, view, or manage options

### Filtering and Sorting

- **Status Filter**: View comics by status (Draft, Published, etc.)
- **Date Sort**: Sort by creation or update date
- **Performance Sort**: Sort by reads, chapters, or earnings
- **Search**: Find specific comics by title

## Status Management

### Understanding Comic Statuses

#### Draft
- **Meaning**: Work in progress, not visible to readers
- **Use Case**: Initial creation and major revisions
- **Actions Available**: Edit, add chapters, request review

#### Requested Review
- **Meaning**: Submitted for platform review
- **Use Case**: Ready for publication consideration
- **Actions Available**: Wait for admin response, edit if needed

#### In Review
- **Meaning**: Being reviewed by platform team
- **Use Case**: Admin is evaluating content
- **Actions Available**: Wait for review completion

#### Published
- **Meaning**: Live and visible to readers
- **Use Case**: Active comic available for reading
- **Actions Available**: Edit, add chapters, monitor performance

#### Unpublished
- **Meaning**: Temporarily removed from public view
- **Use Case**: Content issues, hiatus, or major revisions
- **Actions Available**: Edit, republish when ready

### Status Workflow

#### From Draft to Published
1. **Complete Content**: Ensure all required information is filled
2. **Add Chapters**: Upload at least one chapter
3. **Request Review**: Submit for platform approval
4. **Wait for Review**: Platform team evaluates content
5. **Address Feedback**: Make any requested changes
6. **Publish**: Comic goes live to readers

#### Managing Published Comics
- **Regular Updates**: Add new chapters consistently
- **Monitor Performance**: Track reads and engagement
- **Respond to Feedback**: Address reader comments and concerns
- **Optimize Content**: Update based on analytics
