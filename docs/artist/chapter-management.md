# Chapter Management

## Overview

Chapters are the building blocks of your comic series. Effective chapter management is crucial for reader engagement, monetization, and overall success. This guide covers everything from creating your first chapter to optimizing performance.

## Creating New Chapters

### Before You Start

#### Chapter Planning
- **Story Structure**: Plan chapter length and pacing
- **Visual Consistency**: Maintain art style and quality
- **Reader Expectations**: Balance cliffhangers with satisfaction
- **Update Schedule**: Plan realistic publishing frequency

#### Required Materials
- **Chapter Images**: High-quality artwork (recommended: 800-1200px width)
- **Chapter Title**: Engaging, descriptive title
- **Chapter Description**: Brief summary or teaser
- **Pricing Strategy**: Free vs. paid chapter decision

### Step 1: Access Chapter Creation

#### From Comic View
1. Navigate to your comic's detail page
2. Click **Add Chapter** button
3. Fill in chapter information

#### From Chapters Section
1. Go to **Chapters** in main menu
2. Click **Create Chapter**
3. Select the comic for this chapter

### Step 2: Basic Information

#### Chapter Number
- **Format**: Numeric (1, 2, 3) or descriptive (Prologue, Epilogue)
- **Consistency**: Use consistent numbering system
- **Special Chapters**: Mark prologue chapters appropriately

#### Chapter Title
- **Length**: Keep under 100 characters
- **Engagement**: Make it compelling and descriptive
- **SEO**: Include relevant keywords naturally
- **Examples**: "The Dragon Awakens" or "Chapter 1: First Contact"

#### Chapter Description
- **Purpose**: Tease content without spoiling
- **Length**: 100-300 characters recommended
- **Content**: Highlight key events or character moments
- **Hook**: End with compelling question or cliffhanger

### Step 3: Content Upload

#### Image Requirements
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, WebP
- **Quality**: High resolution, clear artwork
- **Size**: Recommended 800-1200px width
- **Consistency**: Maintain aspect ratio across images

#### Upload Process
1. **Drag and Drop**: Upload images directly to the interface
2. **File Selection**: Choose files from your device
3. **Bulk Upload**: Select multiple images at once
4. **Reordering**: Arrange images in correct reading order

#### Image Organization
- **Reading Order**: Ensure images flow logically
- **Page Breaks**: Consider natural chapter breaks
- **Quality Check**: Review each image before publishing

### Step 4: Monetization Settings

#### Free vs. Paid Chapters
- **Free Chapters**: Attract readers and build audience
- **Paid Chapters**: Generate revenue from engaged readers
- **Strategy**: Balance accessibility with monetization

#### Coin Pricing
- **Range**: 1-50 coins per chapter
- **Factors to Consider**:
  - Chapter length and quality
  - Story importance
  - Reader engagement
  - Market competition
- **Testing**: Start with moderate pricing and adjust

#### Prologue Chapters
- **Purpose**: Special chapters accessible via direct links
- **Use Case**: Bonus content, character introductions
- **Pricing**: Usually free to attract readers
- **Discovery**: Not shown in regular chapter lists

### Step 5: Advanced Settings

#### Language and Localization
- **Primary Language**: Set chapter language
- **Translation**: Consider multiple language versions
- **Audience**: Target specific language communities

#### Content Rating
- **Age Appropriateness**: Ensure accurate content rating
- **Adult Content**: Mark mature content appropriately
- **Platform Guidelines**: Follow content policies

#### SEO and Discovery
- **Keywords**: Include relevant search terms
- **Meta Description**: Brief summary for search results
- **Tags**: Add relevant content tags

## Managing Existing Chapters

### Chapter List View

#### Available Information
- **Chapter Number**: Sequential or descriptive numbering
- **Title**: Chapter name
- **Status**: Current publication status
- **Reads**: Number of times read
- **Earnings**: Revenue generated
- **Actions**: Edit, view, or manage options

#### Filtering Options
- **By Comic**: View chapters for specific comics
- **By Status**: Filter by publication status
- **By Performance**: Sort by reads or earnings
- **By Date**: Sort by creation or update date

### Editing Chapters

#### Content Updates
- **Title Changes**: Update for clarity or engagement
- **Description Updates**: Reflect actual content
- **Image Replacements**: Improve quality or fix errors
- **Reordering**: Adjust image sequence

#### Settings Modifications
- **Pricing Changes**: Adjust coin cost based on performance
- **Status Updates**: Change publication status
- **Category Adjustments**: Update content tags
- **Access Settings**: Modify free/paid status

### Chapter Status Management

#### Understanding Statuses

##### Draft
- **Meaning**: Work in progress, not visible to readers
- **Use Case**: Initial creation and major revisions
- **Actions**: Edit, add images, request review

##### Requested Review
- **Meaning**: Submitted for platform review
- **Use Case**: Ready for publication consideration
- **Actions**: Wait for admin response

##### In Review
- **Meaning**: Being reviewed by platform team
- **Use Case**: Admin evaluating content quality
- **Actions**: Wait for review completion

##### Published
- **Meaning**: Live and visible to readers
- **Use Case**: Active chapter available for reading
- **Actions**: Monitor performance, edit if needed

##### Unpublished
- **Meaning**: Temporarily removed from public view
- **Use Case**: Content issues or major revisions
- **Actions**: Edit, republish when ready

#### Status Workflow
1. **Create**: Start with Draft status
2. **Review**: Ensure quality and completeness
3. **Submit**: Request review when ready
4. **Publish**: Goes live after approval
5. **Monitor**: Track performance and engagement
