# Review Process

## Overview

The review process ensures quality content on the platform while protecting both artists and readers. This guide explains how to submit your content for review, understand the review workflow, and respond to feedback effectively.

## Understanding the Review System

### Why Reviews Are Required

#### Quality Assurance
- **Content Standards**: Ensure all content meets platform guidelines
- **Reader Protection**: Prevent inappropriate or low-quality content
- **Artist Support**: Provide feedback to improve content quality
- **Platform Integrity**: Maintain high standards across all content

#### Review Types

##### Comic Reviews
- **When Required**: Before first publication
- **Scope**: Overall comic quality, cover art, description
- **Focus**: Content appropriateness, visual quality, story coherence

##### Chapter Reviews
- **When Required**: Before chapter publication
- **Scope**: Individual chapter content and quality
- **Focus**: Art quality, story progression, technical standards

### Review Criteria

#### Content Quality
- **Art Standards**: High-quality, clear artwork
- **Story Coherence**: Logical narrative progression
- **Character Development**: Engaging, well-developed characters
- **Pacing**: Appropriate story rhythm and flow

#### Platform Guidelines
- **Age Appropriateness**: Content matches stated rating
- **Community Standards**: Respectful, non-harmful content
- **Copyright Compliance**: Original or properly licensed content
- **Technical Requirements**: Proper formatting and file quality

## Requesting Reviews

### Preparing for Review

#### Before Submitting
1. **Complete Content**: Ensure all required information is filled
2. **Quality Check**: Review artwork and story quality
3. **Guideline Compliance**: Verify content meets platform standards
4. **Technical Requirements**: Check file formats and sizes

#### Required Elements

##### For Comics
- **Complete Profile**: Title, description, categories
- **Visual Assets**: Cover, main, and thumbnail images
- **At Least One Chapter**: Must have content to review
- **Proper Settings**: Adult content, language, status

##### For Chapters
- **Complete Information**: Title, description, chapter number
- **High-Quality Images**: Clear, properly formatted artwork
- **Appropriate Content**: Matches comic rating and description
- **Technical Quality**: Proper file sizes and formats

### Submitting for Review

#### Comic Review Request

1. **Navigate to Comic**: Go to your comic's detail page
2. **Check Requirements**: Ensure all elements are complete
3. **Request Review**: Click "Request Review" button
4. **Confirmation**: Receive confirmation of submission
5. **Wait for Response**: Platform team will review content

#### Chapter Review Request

1. **Navigate to Chapter**: Go to chapter detail page
2. **Verify Content**: Check all information and images
3. **Request Review**: Click "Request Review" button
4. **Confirmation**: Receive submission confirmation
5. **Wait for Response**: Review team will evaluate content

### Review Status Tracking

#### Understanding Status Changes

##### Requested Review
- **Meaning**: Content submitted for review
- **Duration**: Typically 1-3 business days
- **Actions**: Wait for admin response, prepare for feedback

##### In Review
- **Meaning**: Admin team actively evaluating content
- **Duration**: Usually 24-48 hours
- **Actions**: Wait for review completion

##### Review Complete
- **Outcome**: Approved or requires changes
- **Next Steps**: Publish if approved, revise if needed

## Review Workflow

### Step-by-Step Process

#### 1. Submission
- Artist submits content for review
- System notifies admin team
- Content enters review queue

#### 2. Initial Assessment
- Admin reviews basic requirements
- Checks for guideline compliance
- Evaluates content quality

#### 3. Detailed Review
- Comprehensive content evaluation
- Art quality assessment
- Story and character analysis

#### 4. Decision
- Approval for publication
- Request for revisions
- Rejection with explanation

#### 5. Notification
- Artist receives review results
- Feedback provided if needed
- Next steps communicated

### Timeline Expectations

#### Standard Review Times
- **Comic Reviews**: 2-3 business days
- **Chapter Reviews**: 1-2 business days
- **Rush Reviews**: Available for urgent content (contact support)

#### Factors Affecting Timeline
- **Content Complexity**: Longer stories may take more time
- **Review Queue**: High volume may extend wait times
- **Revision Cycles**: Multiple revisions extend total time
- **Platform Events**: Special events may affect review capacity

## Responding to Review Feedback

### Understanding Feedback

#### Common Review Comments

##### Content Quality
- **Art Improvements**: Suggestions for visual enhancements
- **Story Development**: Recommendations for narrative improvement
- **Character Consistency**: Notes on character portrayal
- **Pacing Issues**: Feedback on story rhythm

##### Technical Issues
- **File Quality**: Image resolution or format problems
- **Upload Issues**: Missing or corrupted files
- **Formatting Problems**: Layout or presentation issues
- **Metadata Issues**: Missing or incorrect information

##### Policy Compliance
- **Content Rating**: Age appropriateness concerns
- **Community Guidelines**: Content policy violations
- **Copyright Issues**: Intellectual property concerns
- **Platform Standards**: Quality or formatting requirements

### Making Revisions

#### Addressing Feedback

##### Art Quality Issues
- **Resolution Problems**: Increase image quality
- **Format Issues**: Convert to supported formats
- **Consistency Problems**: Maintain visual style
- **Technical Problems**: Fix upload or display issues

##### Content Improvements
- **Story Enhancements**: Improve narrative elements
- **Character Development**: Strengthen character portrayal
- **Pacing Adjustments**: Optimize story flow
- **Engagement Factors**: Increase reader interest

##### Policy Compliance
- **Content Rating**: Adjust for appropriate audience
- **Guideline Adherence**: Remove problematic content
- **Copyright Resolution**: Ensure proper licensing
- **Platform Standards**: Meet quality requirements

#### Resubmitting Content

1. **Make Revisions**: Address all feedback points
2. **Quality Check**: Review changes before resubmission
3. **Resubmit**: Request review again
4. **Wait for Response**: Allow time for re-evaluation
5. **Iterate if Needed**: Continue until approved

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Review Delays

#### Causes
- **High Volume**: Many submissions in queue
- **Complex Content**: Longer or more complex reviews needed
- **Technical Issues**: Platform or system problems
- **Staff Availability**: Limited reviewer capacity

#### Solutions
- **Plan Ahead**: Submit content well before deadlines
- **Contact Support**: Inquire about delays if excessive
- **Consider Timing**: Avoid peak submission periods
- **Be Patient**: Allow standard review times

### Rejection Handling

#### Common Reasons
- **Quality Issues**: Content doesn't meet standards
- **Policy Violations**: Content violates platform guidelines
- **Technical Problems**: File or formatting issues
- **Incomplete Submissions**: Missing required elements

#### Response Strategies
- **Understand Feedback**: Carefully read rejection reasons
- **Make Improvements**: Address all identified issues
- **Seek Clarification**: Ask for specific guidance if needed
- **Resubmit Confidently**: Ensure quality before resubmission

### Appeal Process

#### When to Appeal
- **Unclear Feedback**: Rejection reasons unclear
- **Disputed Decisions**: Believe content meets standards
- **Technical Errors**: System or process problems
- **Policy Questions**: Need clarification on guidelines

#### Appeal Process
1. **Contact Support**: Reach out through ticket system
2. **Provide Details**: Explain specific concerns
3. **Include Evidence**: Reference relevant guidelines or examples
4. **Be Professional**: Maintain respectful communication
5. **Accept Decision**: Respect final determination
