# Library Management

## Overview

Your personal library is where you organize, track, and manage all your comics. This guide covers how to effectively manage your reading collection, track progress, and organize your favorite content.

## Accessing Your Library

### Library Section

#### Main Library Page
1. **Navigate to Library**: Click "Library" in the main navigation
2. **View Collections**: See all your organized comics
3. **Manage Content**: Add, remove, and organize comics
4. **Track Progress**: Monitor your reading status

#### Library Tabs
- **Reading History**: Comics and chapters you've read
- **Favorites**: Comics you've saved to your collection
- **Purchased**: Content you've bought with coins
- **Bookmarks**: Specific pages you've bookmarked

## Reading History

### Tracking Your Reading

#### Automatic Tracking
- **Read Chapters**: Automatically records completed chapters
- **Reading Time**: Tracks time spent reading each comic
- **Last Read**: Remembers where you stopped reading
- **Progress Percentage**: Shows completion status

#### Reading History Features
- **Chronological Order**: Sorted by most recent activity
- **Comic Information**: Title, author, and chapter details
- **Reading Date**: When you last read each comic
- **Progress Indicators**: Visual progress bars and completion status

### Managing Reading History

#### Viewing History
1. **Open Library**: Navigate to your library section
2. **Select History Tab**: Click on "Reading History"
3. **Browse Comics**: Scroll through your reading history
4. **Filter Options**: Sort by date, title, or progress

#### History Management
- **Remove Items**: Delete comics from your history
- **Clear All**: Remove entire reading history

- **Privacy Settings**: Control history visibility

### Reading Progress

#### Progress Tracking
- **Chapter Progress**: Track which chapters you've read
- **Series Progress**: Overall completion of multi-chapter comics
- **Reading Streaks**: Consecutive days of reading
- **Time Tracking**: Monitor reading time and patterns

#### Progress Features
- **Resume Reading**: Continue where you left off
- **Progress Sync**: Sync across all devices

## Favorites Collection

### Adding Comics to Favorites

#### Adding Favorites
1. **Find a Comic**: Browse or search for comics
2. **View Comic Page**: Open the comic's information page
3. **Click Favorite**: Use the heart or star button
4. **Confirm Addition**: Comic appears in your favorites

#### Favorite Features
- **Quick Access**: Easy access to your favorite comics
- **Organized Collection**: All favorites in one place
- **Update Notifications**: Get notified of new chapters
- **Sharing Options**: Share your favorites with friends

### Managing Favorites

#### Organizing Favorites
- **Sort Options**: Sort by title, author, or date added
- **Search Favorites**: Find specific comics quickly
- **Bulk Actions**: Select multiple comics for actions
- **Custom Lists**: Create themed collections

#### Favorite Management
- **Remove Favorites**: Unfavorite comics you no longer want
- **Move to Lists**: Organize favorites into custom lists

- **Share Collections**: Share favorite lists with others

## Purchased Content

### Tracking Purchases

#### Purchase History
- **Coin Purchases**: Comics bought with coins
- **Purchase Date**: When you bought each comic
- **Cost Information**: How many coins were spent
- **Access Status**: Current access to purchased content

#### Purchase Management
- **View Purchases**: See all your bought content
- **Download Access**: Access purchased content offline
- **Purchase Receipts**: View transaction history
- **Refund Information**: Understand refund policies

### Managing Purchased Content

#### Access Control
- **Permanent Access**: Keep access to purchased content
- **Download Rights**: Download purchased chapters
- **Cross-Device Access**: Access on all your devices
- **Offline Reading**: Read without internet connection

#### Purchase Organization
- **Sort by Date**: Organize by purchase date
- **Sort by Cost**: Arrange by coin cost
- **Filter by Type**: Separate chapters from full comics
- **Search Purchases**: Find specific purchased content

## Bookmarks

### Creating Bookmarks

#### Adding Bookmarks
1. **While Reading**: Navigate to a specific page
2. **Click Bookmark**: Use the bookmark button
3. **Add Notes**: Include personal notes (optional)
4. **Save Bookmark**: Bookmark is added to your collection

#### Bookmark Features
- **Page-Specific**: Mark exact pages for later reference
- **Personal Notes**: Add thoughts or reminders
- **Quick Access**: Jump directly to bookmarked pages
- **Cross-Chapter**: Bookmarks work across all chapters

### Managing Bookmarks

#### Bookmark Organization
- **By Comic**: Group bookmarks by comic title
- **By Chapter**: Organize by chapter number
- **By Date**: Sort by when bookmarks were created
- **By Notes**: Search bookmarks by your notes

#### Bookmark Actions
- **Edit Notes**: Update bookmark descriptions
- **Remove Bookmarks**: Delete unwanted bookmarks

- **Share Bookmarks**: Share with other readers
