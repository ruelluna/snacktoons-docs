# Referral System

## Overview

The referral system allows you to earn rewards by inviting friends to join the platform. Share your referral link, track your referrals, and earn rewards when your friends sign up and become active users.

## Understanding Referrals

### How Referrals Work

#### Referral Process
1. **Get Referral Link**: Access your unique referral link
2. **Share with Friends**: Send your link to friends and family
3. **Friend Signs Up**: They register using your referral link
4. **Earn Rewards**: Receive rewards when they become active users

#### Referral Benefits
- **Earn Cherry Points**: Get points for successful referrals
- **Build Community**: Help friends discover great comics
- **Earn Payouts**: Convert points to real money
- **Track Progress**: Monitor your referral success

### Referral Rewards

#### Reward Structure
- **Sign-up Bonus**: Points when friend creates account
- **Activity Bonus**: Additional points when friend becomes active
- **Payout Eligibility**: Convert points to cash rewards
- **Tier Rewards**: Earn more for multiple successful referrals

#### Reward Types
- **Cherry Points**: Platform currency for rewards
- **Cash Payouts**: Real money for successful referrals
- **Exclusive Content**: Access to special comics
- **Platform Benefits**: Enhanced features and perks
